## Evidence boundary

Public seed/graph/layout fixtures are deterministic synthetic game data. The independent source fixture checks verify captured source facts; they are paired above with executable product assertions and corruption/rejection tests rather than credited alone as gameplay implementation. Setup-source and longest-route fixture provenance are retained in `tests/support/`.

Nonactions are grounded in the exhaustive21 native Command variants plus the strict wire catalogue. There is no delete/refund/downgrade, gift/credit/service/dev-card exchange, manual VP effect, free-road forfeiture, score/dice submission or reservation API. This is an honest structural exclusion, not a fabricated test name. Resource/term/card timing rejections exercise the nearest real boundary. Malformed properties are covered by the completed T30 tests; cross-target/Wasm, performance and deployment are outside this conformance map.
